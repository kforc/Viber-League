## Key bug found + fixed (gameplay stall)
With a strong, well-positioned opponent the chaser parked **0.7 m** from the
carrier, but the shoot condition required `pressure > 0.9` → the carrier could
never shoot. If no forward pass existed it dribbled forever (0-0 matches).
Fixes in `ai.ts` / `engine.ts`:
- Shoot whenever in range unless smothered AND a pass exists.
- `bestPassOption` now allows lateral/short back passes (outlet always exists).
- Chaser stands off at 1.15 m instead of 0.7 m.
- A carried ball can now cross the goal line (no more stalling on the line).

## Round 2 — accurate Viber art, clean graphics, visual verification

Acted on the user's follow-up ("characters aren't accurate", "graphics not clean
focused"). The reference images are `~/Downloads/vibe-vibers-*.png` (60 files).

### Design language extracted from the reference art
A Viber is: **one giant box head (~1.7x torso width, ~half total height)**, a flat
overhanging head-top slab (or rods/antennae/ears/spikes/block/fin), one of four
face treatments, a small torso with a belt slab + chest plate, thin arms with
light cuffs, short legs, **big flat boots**, and **thick uniform black outlines
on every edge**.

Four face treatments (`FaceStyle`): `cubes` (two bare cubes on the head front),
`panel` (coloured plate + two big white eyes with dark pupils), `plate` (large
black plate + two small glowing eyes), `bar` (dark plate + one glowing bar).

### Files rewritten
- `src/sim/roster.ts` — new `ViberDesign` vocabulary: `head / top / face / eye /
  pupil / body / arm / leg / boot / belt / accent + faceStyle + headTop + brow +
  headScale + backpack + tail + ghost`. All 19 Vibers re-authored against the
  reference. Names/roles/attrs/specials and all helpers (overallOf, buildLineup,
  autoOpponent) unchanged.
- `src/render/characters.ts` — rig rebuilt to reference proportions. **Outline
  technique: inflated back-face hull with a FIXED thickness per axis**
  (`scale = (dim + T)/dim`, T = 0.07 rig units) so thin parts get the same border
  as big ones. `LineBasicMaterial` ignores `linewidth` in WebGL, so
  `EdgesGeometry`/`LineSegments` produced hairline outlines — do NOT go back to
  it for this look. Materials: `MeshLambertMaterial` (flat, clean) + unlit
  `MeshBasicMaterial` for glowing eyes. `ROOT_SCALE = 0.56` (~1.9 world units).
- `src/ui/avatar.ts` + the `CSS MINI-VIBER AVATAR` block in `src/style.css` —
  rewritten to the same vocabulary (`.vv-ht-*`, `.vv-face-cubes|panel|plate|bar`).
- `src/render/renderer.ts` — added `THREE.Fog` from the weather preset, softer
  lights (hemi 0.62 / sun 1.3 for face-to-face contrast), camera lowered to
  `CAM_HEIGHT 25 / CAM_BACK 29` so faces are visible, ball → Lambert.
- `src/render/pitch.ts` — Lambert materials, slightly less neon green
  (`#3d9e55`), surround `0x1e2a44`.
- `src/sim/weather.ts` — added `fogNear` / `fogFar` per preset.

## Late-session update — design vocabulary + balance

### Concurrent edit (important)

`src/style.css` were **rewritten in parallel** to a richer design vocabulary.

```
headTop:   'none' | 'flatslab' | 'mortar' | 'rods' | 'antenna'
         | 'ears' | 'spikes' | 'block' | 'wing'
brow?, headScale?, backpack?, tail?, ghost?
```

`render/characters.ts` now uses **EdgesGeometry + LineSegments** outlines (not
inverted hull), MeshLambertMaterial flat shading, a GIANT head (1.72 vs 1.0
torso width) and `ROOT_SCALE = 0.56`. 19 Vibers in the roster.

### Balance work (the real win)
Symptom: a hand-picked elite five beat a rating-matched opponent **56-7**.

Diagnostics run (temporary `tests/dump.test.ts`, since deleted):
- **Mirror squads (same five both sides)**: near-even → **no structural side
  bias**. Confirmed by swapping sides: the strong squad won 56 as home and 72 as
  away, so the margin is squad quality, not the side.
- The auto-opponent was correctly rating-matched (76-78 vs 77) — yet lost 9:1.

Root cause: **`overallOf` did not reflect what the sim rewards.** Specifically
`shooting` was weighted 0.30 for FWD but had **no effect on shot accuracy** at
all (spread was purely distance-based).

Fixes:
1. `engine.ts` — shot spread now scales with the shooter's skill:
   `skill = 1.25 - (shooting/99) * 0.55`, so elite finishers are ~35% tighter.
2. `roster.ts` — reweighted `overallOf` to match the sim: pace and strength up,
   shooting down (FWD 0.22/0.22/0.16; MID 0.20/0.18/0.08; DEF 0.18/0.30/0.02).
3. `roster.ts` — `autoOpponent` now builds strongest-first per position and, if
   the distinct pool still rates below the player, **tops up the weakest slot
   from the full roster** until the ratings match.

Result: strong-squad total **125 → 76**, weak-squad total **14 → 25**; typical
scorelines are now 2-3 / 3-4 / 2-1 / 4-3 instead of 11-0.

### Roster: 19 → 59 Vibers

6 via the Read tool. New names: BEACON, WARDEN, TANGERINE, INDIGO, SPRUCE,
MAROON, SPLIT, SOUFFLE, MEDIC, VISOR, BUBBLE, RIVET, CHEF, FEDORA, PLUM, AMBER,
SEAFOAM, GLIDER, MAUVE, PORTER, PERISCOPE, HONEY, SCOPE, SAIL, BRACKET, MUSTARD,
ICICLE, MAGNET, AZURE, MOOSE, MIDNIGHT, NAVY, RANGER, SHERIFF, SOLDER, GLACIER,
CACTUS, VANE, HEDGE, CRANE. No id collisions; all six attribute keys in 1..99.

### Set pieces — new `src/sim/setpiece.ts`
- **The arena boards are gone.** `physics.integrateBall` now returns
  `{ goal, out }` and clamps the ball to the painted line instead of bouncing.
  `awardFromOutOfPlay` decides the restart:
  touchline → throw-in; goal line + last touch by the defender → **corner**;
  goal line + last touch by the attacker → goal kick.
- `awardFoul` → **penalty** if the failed tackler is in their own box, else free kick.
- `MatchPhase` gained `throwin | corner | goalkick | freekick | penalty`, and
  `World.setPiece` holds `{ kind, team, pos, taker, side }`.
- **Corners and penalties snap everyone to their station** (`snapToStations`).
  Without this the corner was taken with nobody in the box — attackers cannot run
  30 m in a 1.1 s pause, so `pickBoxTarget` kept returning the taker itself and
  the "cross" went straight down the goal line.
- `Ball.saveCooldown` gives the keeper **one dive per shot**. Without it the
  keeper's 2.5 m control radius covered the whole 4 m goal and *every* shot was
  claimed (0-0 matches). Shots now resolve as held / tipped behind for a corner /
  goal. Outfield blocks of fast balls deflect behind for corners too.

### Tactics
- Carriers **cross** when wide, advanced, and a teammate is actually in the box.
- Support positioning spreads midfielders wider (they hold the touchline).
- Dribble target drives at the goal again; only a very wide + very deep carrier
  holds the corner. (An earlier "always drive to the corner flag" version removed
  the carried-ball goals and produced 0-0 matches.)
- `bestPassOption` weights progression much higher (forward ×1.35) so play stops
  going sideways; shoot on sight inside 6 m.
- Failed tackles roll for a foul (`FOUL_CHANCE_BOX` / `FOUL_CHANCE_OPEN`).

### Bugs found and fixed along the way
- `deflectBehind` pushed the ball *into* the field: team 0 defends **-x**, so
  "towards their own goal" is -x for them. This is why corners stayed at 0.
- A restart left pending when the clock expired resurrected the match after
  full time (two `fulltime` events). Guard: `w.phase === w.setPiece.kind`.
- `dribbleBall` could carry the ball off the pitch; now clamped, with the goal
  mouth as the only exception (so walked-in goals still work).
- The clock now runs through dead balls, so the chosen match length stays exact.

### Balance (3-minute match, 6-match season sample)
~2.8 goals, 18.5 shots, 7.3 corners, 4.7 free kicks, 1.3 throw-ins per match.
Mirror-squad test: home 16 / away 14 over 8 seeds → no structural side bias.
