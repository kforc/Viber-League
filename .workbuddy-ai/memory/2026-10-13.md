# 2026-10-13 — league from scratch, twelve-man squads, injuries and bans

## What shipped

League mode now starts from an empty table (`New league` on the League screen,
two-tap confirm), and the manager names **up to 12 Vibers** for league games.
The first five FIT players start, the rest cover. Injuries and red cards happen
in a match and cost the player fixtures.

- **Absences belong to the PLAYER, not to a squad slot.** `CareerState.absences`
  is keyed by roster id, so dropping someone does not heal them — otherwise
  "drop + re-add" is a two-tap cure. `autoPick` was rewritten to top the squad
  up without releasing anyone, which was the other half of that loophole.
- **Determinism held twice over.** Cards and injuries come off a SECOND seeded
  stream (`World.disciplineRng`), so a booking cannot shift the match rng; and
  the number of draws per foul is kept constant (the ban is rolled even for a
  yellow, then discarded) so a yellow cannot shift it either. `discipline.test.ts`
  pins both.
- Severity "Moderate" (user's choice): injuries 1–3 fixtures, red cards 1–2.
  Substitutions stay at 3 (user's choice).
- A sent-off or injured player is parked off the pitch and skipped everywhere:
  starting XI, bench, AI targeting, possession, set-piece takers, collisions.

## The interesting part: the harness was lying

The landscape harness reported **16 failures** after the feature. Fifteen were
one cascade: the match never reached half-time or full time. Root cause was not
the game at all —

**Headless Chrome has no compositor.** rAF runs at ~5fps, the app clamps a frame
to 50ms and takes ≤6 ticks, so the sim advances at **0.25× realtime**. A 90s half
is ~6 minutes of wall clock and the harness budgeted 300s. The match had been
playing perfectly the whole time (the `17-result` screenshot showed 13' and a
live mentality row). Worse, the failure rate tracked machine load, so a green run
said more about the CPU than about the game.

`tools/cdp/probe-rate.mjs` measured it (48 samples, 0 stalls, 0.25×). The fix:
`flow-club.mjs` now injects a bundled `TestSim` via
`Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument` and **steps the real world directly**,
the way `probe-penalty.mjs` always has — and answers the penalty and half-time
panels the app raises, which the harness had never done (a penalty would have
hung it outright). Run time 15 min → 3 min, and it no longer depends on load.

## Two real bugs the screenshots found

Both were invisible to assertions and only showed up in the rendered PNG:

1. **The card rail was clipping the cards top AND bottom.** The new squad strip
   took ~48px straight off the rail, so the rating plates were sliced in half and
   the role bars cut off. Fixed by making the landscape strip two rows instead of
   three (legend on its own line, both groups sharing the next) and reclaiming a
   few px from the chrome and the card. `flow-club.mjs` now reports the rail/card
   rects on failure instead of just a pixel count.
2. **`.viber-card2.selected` had no opaque layer.** Its background was the role
   glow over `rgba(223,249,2,0.1)` and nothing else, so the chosen card was 90%
   transparent — the giant 3D hero model and the pitch sat on top of the player's
   name and role bar. Only ever on the SELECTED card. Fixed by keeping
   `var(--panel-2)` at the bottom of the stack.

Also: the portrait strip truncated reserve names to "CHIM…", "GLAC…", "SOUP…".
The reserve row now wraps at a 64px floor (longest Viber is TANGERINE), while
the starting five stays one row of five.

# Phase B — the league starts level (user bug report)

The user sent a screenshot of the League table: their club on **0 played / 0
points** while every CPU club showed **10 played / 25 points** — before they had
kicked off. Their words: *"the league has to start on a clean level for all. No
one starts a new league with points and stats. Everyone should have 0s while
they all play their respective matches."*

## Root cause

`completeResults()` in `src/sim/league.ts` filled in the **whole** CPU season the
moment any table was built. The player's own 12 fixtures are persisted; every
CPU-vs-CPU result is *derived on demand*. So building the table at season start
simulated all 30 CPU fixtures at once.

Found a latent second bug while in there: `summariseSeason()` built its table
from the raw stored results, which hold **only the player's fixtures** — leaving
every CPU club on zero. That made the player top of the league by default, so
**every season ever filed recorded the player as CHAMPION** regardless of how
they played. Fixed by completing the season before reading the table.

## The reveal rule (the interesting part)

A seven-club league rests one club per round, and the circle method hands the
bye to `ids[0]` — the player — in round 0. The player's fixtures are therefore
rounds `1-6, 8-13`; they sit out 0 and 7.

Measured three candidate rules over all 12 matchdays rather than guessing:

| rule | result |
|---|---|
| A: every round up to the player's last | player **1 game behind** the pack on *every* matchday |
| B: the first *n* rounds | mixes both, spread up to 2 |
| **I: only the rounds the player actually played** | player **never behind** (`ahead=0` at every n) |

**A reproduces the reported bug in miniature** — after the player's first match
they would see five clubs on 2 games and themselves on 1. Chose **I**: the
player's fixture list IS the calendar, so after matchday one six clubs and the
player sit on 1 game and only the bye club is on 0.

## Also

- `playerStrength` was removed from `completeResults`' opts — it was never used
  (CPU fixtures never involve the player), and both call sites in `game.ts` plus
  the now-unused `teamOverall` import had to go.
- `tests/sim/league.test.ts`: `completeResults` block rewritten for lockstep,
  plus a property test asserting the player is tied with the busiest club on
  **every** matchday 1..12, and a regression test that a lost season does not
  file the player as champion.
- **The harness never asserted the table's numbers** — only its structure (row
  count, header, clipping). A table showing six clubs 10 games in looked
  perfectly well-formed. Added `readTable()` to `flow-club.mjs` and three checks:
  a fresh league is all zeros; nobody has played more than the player; only the
  bye club is on 0.
