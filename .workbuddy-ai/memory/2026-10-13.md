# 2026-10-13 — league from scratch, twelve-man squads, injuries and bans

## What shipped

League mode now starts from an empty table (`New league` on the League screen,
two-tap confirm), and the manager names **up to 12 Vibers** for league games.
The first five FIT players start, the rest cover. Injuries and red cards happen
in a match and cost the player fixtures.

- **Absences belong to the PLAYER, not to a squad slot.** `CareerState.absences`
  is keyed by roster id, so dropping someone does not heal them — otherwise
  "drop + re-add" is a two-tap cure. `autoPick` was rewritten to top the squad
  up without releasing anyone, which was the other half of that loophole.
- **Determinism held twice over.** Cards and injuries come off a SECOND seeded
  stream (`World.disciplineRng`), so a booking cannot shift the match rng; and
  the number of draws per foul is kept constant (the ban is rolled even for a
  yellow, then discarded) so a yellow cannot shift it either. `discipline.test.ts`
  pins both.
- Severity "Moderate" (user's choice): injuries 1–3 fixtures, red cards 1–2.
  Substitutions stay at 3 (user's choice).
- A sent-off or injured player is parked off the pitch and skipped everywhere:
  starting XI, bench, AI targeting, possession, set-piece takers, collisions.

## The interesting part: the harness was lying

The landscape harness reported **16 failures** after the feature. Fifteen were
one cascade: the match never reached half-time or full time. Root cause was not
the game at all —

**Headless Chrome has no compositor.** rAF runs at ~5fps, the app clamps a frame
to 50ms and takes ≤6 ticks, so the sim advances at **0.25× realtime**. A 90s half
is ~6 minutes of wall clock and the harness budgeted 300s. The match had been
playing perfectly the whole time (the `17-result` screenshot showed 13' and a
live mentality row). Worse, the failure rate tracked machine load, so a green run
said more about the CPU than about the game.

`tools/cdp/probe-rate.mjs` measured it (48 samples, 0 stalls, 0.25×). The fix:
`flow-club.mjs` now injects a bundled `TestSim` via
`Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument` and **steps the real world directly**,
the way `probe-penalty.mjs` always has — and answers the penalty and half-time
panels the app raises, which the harness had never done (a penalty would have
hung it outright). Run time 15 min → 3 min, and it no longer depends on load.

## Two real bugs the screenshots found

Both were invisible to assertions and only showed up in the rendered PNG:

1. **The card rail was clipping the cards top AND bottom.** The new squad strip
   took ~48px straight off the rail, so the rating plates were sliced in half and
   the role bars cut off. Fixed by making the landscape strip two rows instead of
   three (legend on its own line, both groups sharing the next) and reclaiming a
   few px from the chrome and the card. `flow-club.mjs` now reports the rail/card
   rects on failure instead of just a pixel count.
2. **`.viber-card2.selected` had no opaque layer.** Its background was the role
   glow over `rgba(223,249,2,0.1)` and nothing else, so the chosen card was 90%
   transparent — the giant 3D hero model and the pitch sat on top of the player's
   name and role bar. Only ever on the SELECTED card. Fixed by keeping
   `var(--panel-2)` at the bottom of the stack.

Also: the portrait strip truncated reserve names to "CHIM…", "GLAC…", "SOUP…".
The reserve row now wraps at a 64px floor (longest Viber is TANGERINE), while
the starting five stays one row of five.

# Phase B — the league starts level (user bug report)

The user sent a screenshot of the League table: their club on **0 played / 0
points** while every CPU club showed **10 played / 25 points** — before they had
kicked off. Their words: *"the league has to start on a clean level for all. No
one starts a new league with points and stats. Everyone should have 0s while
they all play their respective matches."*

## Root cause

`completeResults()` in `src/sim/league.ts` filled in the **whole** CPU season the
moment any table was built. The player's own 12 fixtures are persisted; every
CPU-vs-CPU result is *derived on demand*. So building the table at season start
simulated all 30 CPU fixtures at once.

Found a latent second bug while in there: `summariseSeason()` built its table
from the raw stored results, which hold **only the player's fixtures** — leaving
every CPU club on zero. That made the player top of the league by default, so
**every season ever filed recorded the player as CHAMPION** regardless of how
they played. Fixed by completing the season before reading the table.

## The reveal rule (the interesting part)

A seven-club league rests one club per round, and the circle method hands the
bye to `ids[0]` — the player — in round 0. The player's fixtures are therefore
rounds `1-6, 8-13`; they sit out 0 and 7.

Measured three candidate rules over all 12 matchdays rather than guessing:

| rule | result |
|---|---|
| A: every round up to the player's last | player **1 game behind** the pack on *every* matchday |
| B: the first *n* rounds | mixes both, spread up to 2 |
| **I: only the rounds the player actually played** | player **never behind** (`ahead=0` at every n) |

**A reproduces the reported bug in miniature** — after the player's first match
they would see five clubs on 2 games and themselves on 1. Chose **I**: the
player's fixture list IS the calendar, so after matchday one six clubs and the
player sit on 1 game and only the bye club is on 0.

## Also

- `playerStrength` was removed from `completeResults`' opts — it was never used
  (CPU fixtures never involve the player), and both call sites in `game.ts` plus
  the now-unused `teamOverall` import had to go.
- `tests/sim/league.test.ts`: `completeResults` block rewritten for lockstep,
  plus a property test asserting the player is tied with the busiest club on
  **every** matchday 1..12, and a regression test that a lost season does not
  file the player as champion.
- **The harness never asserted the table's numbers** — only its structure (row
  count, header, clipping). A table showing six clubs 10 games in looked
  perfectly well-formed. Added `readTable()` to `flow-club.mjs` and three checks:
  a fresh league is all zeros; nobody has played more than the player; only the
  bye club is on 0.

- `tsc --noEmit` clean. **235 tests green** (league 36, career 58, profile 27,
  sound 20, brand 21, celebration 12, discipline 11, gameplay 7, setpiece 14,
  subs 11, tactics-shape 3, match 15).
- Both CDP harnesses green: landscape **0 failures**, portrait **0 failures**.
- Bundle 763 kB, self-contained, 0 external refs.
- Confirmed against the committed HEAD screenshot: the same screen used to show

# Phase C — three game modes (Quick Play, League, PvP deferred)

The user asked for three modes behind one PLAY button, and asked for an opinion
**before** any code. After that exchange the decisions were:

- **PvP: not built.** "Build modes 1 & 2 first."
- **Quick Play: fully isolated** — no result filed, no injuries, no ban served.
- **Subs: 3 per match, unchanged.**

## The design: a mode is a lens, not a fork

There is ONE pre-match flow (squad → venue → minutes → opponent → kick off). The
mode changes *what the flow reads*, not which flow runs. That is carried by a
single accessor:

```ts
private get activeSelected(): string[] {
  return this.mode === 'quick' ? this.quickSelected : this.selected;
}
```

Everything downstream — `starters()`, `commitLineup()`, `refreshBench()`,
`togglePick()`, `autoPick()`, the strip, the detail sheet — goes through it, so
Quick Play isolation is structural rather than a scatter of `if (quick)` guards.

`starters()` short-circuits for quick and returns `quickSelected.slice(0, 5)`
without consulting `fitSquad(this.career)` — a friendly must not inherit the
league's absences.

## The bug that mattered: career writes were outside the fixture guard

In `enterResult`, the absence block was:

```ts
next = withAbsences(tickAbsences(next), picked);
this.commitCareer(next);
```

…sitting OUTSIDE `if (this.fixture)`. A friendly has no fixture, so it would have
**injured league players and served their bans** — and nothing on screen would
have said so. All career writes are now gated on `!quick`, and `picked` is `{}`
for quick so `tickAbsences` is never even reached. This is the exact class of bug
the mode screen's own copy warns about ("Nothing recorded").

Also fixed while wiring: `openSection()` now claims `this.mode = 'league'`, so
tapping the title's League tile after a friendly does not render the league using
the *quick* five.

## The harness lesson (the important one)

My first version of the quick-play section drove the friendly with
`runMatchTo('fulltime', …)` and asserted `exists('.overlay-screen')`.

Both were wrong, and together they produced a **green-looking section that tested
nothing**:

1. A match stops at half-time for a mentality. Driving straight to full time
   spins against a world parked in `halftime`, and the guard gives up.
2. `.overlay-screen` matches the **half-time panel too**, so "reaches full time"
   passed on the wrong panel.

The friendly was stuck in the first half while the section reported success — and
the three isolation checks below it (results/absences/lineup unchanged) were
**vacuous**: no full-time whistle had ever been reached for the career to fail to
record. Fix: drive it in two halves like the league flow, answer the mentality,
and assert `.stats-table` — the one thing that tells the result panel from the
half-time one.

Generalised: **a check that passes on the wrong screen is worse than no check.**

## A real bug the screenshots caught: `0.06666666666666667 min`

`renderOpponent`'s conditions strip inlined `${(halfSeconds * 2) / 60} min`.
Every length the player can *pick* is a whole number of minutes, so it looked
fine — but the harness pins `halfSeconds: 2` for speed, and `(2 * 2) / 60` is
`0.0666…`, printed raw.

**The HUD already had the fix.** `hud.ts` grew a `displayLength()` guard when
this same string was caught before, with a comment naming it. The venue screen
kept the bare expression and kept the bug. Now `displayLength` lives in
`screens.ts` beside `LENGTH_OPTIONS` (the match-length vocabulary), is exported,
and `hud.ts` imports it — one definition, so the two strips cannot drift. The
HUD's format moved from `3min` to `3 min` to match; nothing asserted the old one.

Pinned by a new test in `career.test.ts` (the file that already pins
`halfSeconds` to `LENGTH_OPTIONS`) and by two harness checks that assert the
strip's **text**, not its chip count — counting the chips is what let this
through.

## Why PvP is not in the build

Three concrete blockers, told to the user rather than hidden:

1. The game is offline-by-design (one HTML file, no server), so there is no
   relay to sync through.
2. `sim/` is deterministic *within one engine*, but it calls `Math.exp`,
   `Math.hypot`, `Math.atan2`, `Math.cos`, `Math.sin` — ECMAScript lets engines
   approximate these, so two devices are not guaranteed bit-identical. Strict
   lockstep needs those replaced with integer/fixed-point math first.
3. It needs two-device testing, which this harness cannot do.

A tile that leads nowhere is worse than no tile, so PvP is absent from the mode
screen and the harness asserts it stays absent.

# Phase D — the front page and the league gate (user request, 4 parts)

The user asked for four things in one message:

1. Pause should show a plain **QUIT** back to the first page, not "QUIT TO LEAGUE".
2. The **SQUAD** and **PLAYERS** tiles should come off the front page.
3. **LEAGUE** must not show a default set of clubs before a league is created —
   route it `League -> NEW LEAGUE`, with `LOAD EXISTING LEAGUE` under it, resuming
   the season the player was already in so **no progress is lost**.
4. Remove the subtle texts "like this" all over the game, and add a **HELP** guide
   instead.

Asked before building; the answers were: subtle text **"everything goes"**,
load-existing **"just resume the current one"**, HELP **"a button on the Play
screen"**, and **"keep the Players tile"** (only SQUAD goes).

## The gate is gated on `leagueStartedAt`, and deliberately not migrated

`CareerState` grew `leagueStartedAt: string | null`. `loadCareer` does NOT
backfill it:

```ts
leagueStartedAt: typeof c.leagueStartedAt === 'string' ? c.leagueStartedAt : null,
```

That is the whole trick. A save written by an earlier build has a season in it
and no marker, so it lands on the gate with its table, fixtures and injuries
intact — which is exactly where **LOAD EXISTING LEAGUE** appears instead of the
player's progress being silently wiped. Backfilling would have hidden the bug
this feature exists to prevent. `career.test.ts` pins it: strip
`leagueStartedAt` from raw storage, reload, assert `leagueExists === false` AND
`hasStoredLeague === true` AND the results survive.

`hasStoredLeague` (results or history non-empty) is what makes the load button
dead-but-present on a fresh club. A *missing* button reads as a feature that was
never built; a disabled one reads as honestly not applicable.

## State moved into chips, not into the bin

"Everything goes" meant every subtitle — including the ones carrying state. The
state itself was kept, restyled as header chips: `Season 1 · 0/12` and the
league position on LEAGUE, `Round 2 · Away` on the opponent screen, `Season N`
on CAREER. A subtitle explains a screen; a chip is a fact about it.

One blocking warning survives on purpose: a disabled PLAY NEXT MATCH with no
reason is a usability bug, not a subtlety.

## Three bugs found while doing it

1. **HELP's BACK skipped a level.** `onHelp` called `this.showHelp()` directly,
   which set `state` but never pushed the screen onto the history stack, so BACK
   popped the entry *below* the Play screen and dropped the player on the title.
   Fixed by routing it through `nav('help')` — which also needed a `case 'help'`
   that did not exist.
2. **`tile paint: null` in the harness.** The CSS diagnostic that measures the
   menu tiles sat one screen past them once LEAGUE opened on the gate. It kept
   logging, and kept measuring nothing. Moved into section 1.
3. **The last `.screen-sub`.** I had been asserting in harness comments that the
   class was gone; it was not — the sign-in card still wore one, restating the
   club name the crest below already shows. Removed, with all five of its CSS
   rules.

## The harness lesson, again: indexes drift, labels do not

`flow-club.mjs` clicked `.menu-opt` by **index**. Dropping the SQUAD tile shifted
every index after it, so `click('.menu-opt', 1)` (Squad) now opens Players and
`click('.menu-opt', 3)` (Career) now opens Settings — a harness that fails by
quietly opening the wrong screen. Every tile click is now `clickText(label)`.

Same family: two assertions checked `'No win on the board yet'`, a string this
pass had shortened to `'No win yet.'`. A stale assertion is worse than none —
it fails for a reason that has nothing to do with the code.

The `.squad-view` checks moved to the result panel's "Squad" shortcut, the only
door left to that screen. **Do not test a screen through a route that no longer
exists.**

# The guide on a pitch, and a misdiagnosis corrected

## The title chip was never overlapped — it was laid out as two rows

The section above records "the framed Viber overlaps the club-crest line" as a
pre-existing defect. **That was wrong, and the wrongness mattered.**

I measured it instead of eyeballing it. `probe-hero-band.mjs` screenshots the
title with and without the hero and diffs them — the figure has no DOM node, so
subtraction is the only way to get its real bounding box. It spans y=163..254,
**entirely inside** the 159..259 band the layout reserved. The Viber was never
over the club name.

The real cause was the cascade trap this file already warns about, in a new
place. `.menu-crest` (line ~1294) sets `display: flex`; `.club-crest` (line
~2328) sets `display: inline-grid`, `height: 22px`, `place-items: center`,
`border-radius: 6px`. Equal specificity (0,1,0), **later declaration wins**, so
the title chip silently inherited the *league table's* two-letter badge style and
laid the badge and the name out as TWO GRID ROWS. The name sat 17px below the
chip's own background — directly under the figure, which is what made it read as
an overlap. It was in HEAD too; I confirmed that before claiming a regression.

Fixed by out-ranking the collateral rule rather than editing it:
`.menu-crest.club-crest { display: flex; height: auto; ... }` plus a badge-size
rule. Chip 22 → 34px tall, band 100 → 88px, figure clear at 175..258.

**The lesson is the diagnostic one.** `textContent` returned "Riverside Rovers"
the whole time, so the harness passed while nothing was painted inside the pill.
When a defect is *spatial*, assert geometry: `flow-club.mjs` now checks the name
is inside the chip, on the same row as the badge, and actually given width to
draw. And when a screenshot suggests one cause, measure before believing it.

## The guide now sits on a pitch

Green board, mown bands, a light-green touchline, white copy. Two passes:

- The first used two greens 0.018 apart in luminance. In the PNG that rendered
  as **one flat field** — the bands were in the DOM and invisible to the eye.
  Widened to `#17612e` / `#0e4620`.
- The centre circle and halfway line sit *behind the copy*, so their opacity is
  a legibility decision, not a taste one. At 0.14 the lightest pixel a glyph can
  land on measures **4.69:1** against the lime titles and **5.62:1** against the
  white lines. Brighter than ~0.16 drops the titles under 4.5:1.

Both numbers were measured off the rendered PNG with Pillow, and the model
predicted the rendered marking colour to within 1–2 levels — worth doing, because
"the text is white" says nothing about what is under it.

The markings get their own absolutely-positioned layer. Drawn on `.help-body`
they scroll away with the first topic.

## The content was wrong in five places

The guide is the only place the removed subtitles live, so a line describing a
game other than this one is a real loss. Checked every claim against `sim/`:

- **SPECIAL** — written as "fires a shot from distance". It is a 6-second,
  ×1.28 **whole-team speed surge** on a 22-second cooldown (`physics.ts`:
  `specialTimer > 0 ? SPECIAL_SPEED_MULT : 1`).
- **Weather** — the section invented **Fog** and **Clear**. `WEATHER_ORDER` is
  `sun / rain / ice / cold`; snow is a precipitation *effect of ICY*, not a
  weather. The friction directions were muddled too. Rewritten to the four
  conditions, using the sim's own blurbs so the guide and the venue screen agree.
- **Injury and ban length** — "the next match or two". `INJURY_MATCHES` is [1,3]
  and `SUSPENSION_MATCHES` [1,2].
- **CLEAR** — the game offers a contextual third action when the carrier is deep
  in their own half. The guide never mentioned it.
- **Penalties** — "pick your side and watch the keeper" cannot happen: the dive
  is decided inside `takePenalty`, at the kick. Rewritten to what the player
  actually does. Also added **Balanced**, the default mentality the guide omitted.

`flow-club.mjs` now pins the corrected facts: the four condition names appear,
no `Fog`, and SPECIAL is not described as a shot.
